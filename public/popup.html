What SEEge records and why

What it records

  • Your real name and when you consented
  • When you join and leave a monitored Meet
  • How long you are away from the Meet tab
  • Counts of chat sends, reactions and microphone openings, when Meet exposes them

What it never records

  • Chat text, audio, video or screenshots
  • Anything you do on other websites
  • Any class you are not rostered in

Sign-in sends your account email to Firebase Authentication. Tracking starts only after you save consent and your name, and only for an active class you are rostered in. Your teacher can see your session activity and send it in a report to their own Gmail. Session data is scheduled for deletion after 90 days, though deletion can be delayed if the teacher's extension is not running. You can request deletion from this popup, or contact the maintainer through the SEEge GitHub repository. Focus is an indicator, not proof of attention.
