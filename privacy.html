Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SEEge handles information when you use the SEEge Chrome extension.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. About SEEge

SEEge is an independent student project designed to help users review attendance-related activity during Google Meet sessions. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

2. Information the extension may process

Depending on the features you use, SEEge may process the following information:

  • Meeting activity: events such as joining, leaving, reconnecting, and detected periods of inactivity.
  • Event timing: timestamps and calculated durations associated with recorded activity.
  • Account information: if Google sign-in is enabled, basic profile information such as your email address and authentication identifier.

SEEge is not designed to record meeting audio or video, read private chat messages, or identify what participants say during a meeting.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Display attendance-related activity and session history.
  • Calculate durations between detected events.
  • Authenticate users when account features are available.
  • Support and improve the extension's functionality.

4. Storage and sharing

The extension may use browser storage and, if implemented and enabled, Firebase services to support its features. The exact information stored remotely depends on the functionality deployed in the version you use.

SEEge does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties for their independent advertising purposes. Service providers may process information as needed to provide features that you use.

5. Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, SEEge uses Google's authentication services to verify your identity. Google handles authentication according to its own privacy policy and terms. SEEge does not ask for your Google password.

You can choose not to sign in, although some features may require authentication.

6. Permissions

SEEge requests browser permissions needed for its functionality, including access to supported Google Meet pages, browser storage, and identity services when applicable. These permissions are used to operate the extension's attendance and account features.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information may remain in browser storage or a connected service for as long as needed for the features you use. Available deletion and retention controls depend on the implemented version. If you need help with information associated with SEEge, use the project repository linked below to contact the developer.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are intended to protect information handled by SEEge. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

SEEge is intended as a general educational utility, not as a service specifically directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that raises privacy concerns, contact the developer.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when SEEge's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, visit the SEEge GitHub repository and contact the project maintainer.

Important: This policy should accurately reflect the extension's actual behavior before public release. Review and update it whenever data collection, storage, sharing, or deletion features change.
